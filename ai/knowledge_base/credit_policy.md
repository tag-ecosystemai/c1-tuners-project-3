# Kredt Consumer Loan Underwriting Policy (CBN Aligned)

## Section 3: Eligibility & Age Criteria
- Rule Ref: POL-ELIG-3.2
- Minimum Age: 18 years old. No natural person under 18 may be approved for credit facilities (Basis: CPR 3.1.1, Kredt Parameter).
- Identification: Borrower must provide a valid government-issued ID and an active Bank Verification Number (BVN).

## Section 4: Customer Due Diligence (CDD) & Verification
- Rule Ref: POL-CDD-4.1
- Mandatory Customer Data: Legal name, permanent address, phone, email, DoB, BVN, TIN, nationality, employment status, employer, and unexpired official ID (Basis: CDD Reg. 6(a)).
- Rule Ref: POL-CDD-4.3
- Identity Discrepancy: Any mismatch between BVN registration details and submitted ID documents must trigger a referral for manual underwriter review and cannot be auto-approved.

## Section 5: Credit Assessment, Affordability & Loan Sizing
- Rule Ref: POL-CRED-5.1
- Credit Reference Check: Enquiries or credit reports from at least two credit bureaus and CRMS database check are required prior to approval (Basis: CPR 5.2.3, PG 3.7(c)).
- Rule Ref: POL-AFF-5.4.1
- Debt-to-Income (DTI) Limit: Total monthly debt obligations including the proposed loan repayment must not exceed 40% of verified net monthly income (Basis: CPR 5.2.2, Kredt Parameter).
- Rule Ref: POL-AFF-5.4.2
- Minimum Disposable Income Floor: After all monthly debt service, the borrower must retain at least NGN 30,000 in unencumbered monthly disposable income (Basis: Kredt Parameter).
- Rule Ref: POL-SIZE-5.5
- Loan Sizing Limit: Requested loan principal cannot exceed 3 times (3x) verified net monthly income (Basis: Kredt Parameter).
- Rule Ref: POL-TENOR-5.5
- Tenor Window: Permitted repayment tenors range from 1 to 24 months.

## Section 6: Disclosures and Transparency
- Rule Ref: POL-DISC-6.1
- Key Facts Statement (KFS): Borrower must be provided with a KFS in CBN Annexure A format and given at least 2 days to review prior to signing (Basis: CPR 4.3.4, CPR 4.3.5).
- Cooling-off Period: 3 working days statutory cancellation window applies after agreement execution (Basis: CPR 4.3.6).
